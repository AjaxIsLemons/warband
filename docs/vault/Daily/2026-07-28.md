## Persistent Warband footer — roster drag and drop

The shell-owned footer can now manipulate the roster instead of only inspecting heroes and
transferring gear.

- `RunController.MoveRosterHero` addresses the source by stable hero identity and the destination
  by zone + visible slot. An open slot moves the hero into that packed roster; an occupied slot
  swaps atomically, including field↔reserve when both zones are full. Planning/spec-choice guards
  remain authoritative in the run layer.
- `WarbandBarView` owns pointer capture, a seven-pixel drag threshold, source/target/ghost states,
  invalid-drop cancellation, and legal highlighting. Green open slots mean move; blue occupied
  slots mean swap. Ordinary clicks still open the dossier.
- Keyboard parity: Space arms a focused hero and places/swaps it on the next legal slot; Space on
  the source or Escape cancels. Tooltips disclose the interaction.
- The footer projection now carries an explicit `SlotIndex`, independent of the field-only index
  used by deployment.
- Added the deterministic `rail-open` fixture, including an editor-only drag preview and roster
  interaction report.

The last P1 blocker on friends playtest #1. Built as **a modal over the shell's persistent
layer** — one implementation serves Menu, Hall and fight — rather than a routed screen, because
mid-fight is where mute and battle speed are actually wanted and routing away from a fight is
worse than a scrim over it.

- **`PlayerOptions.cs`** (new): PlayerPrefs store; the only new state. Sound on/off,
  Master/Interface/Battle volumes, reduced motion (reuses the existing `ui.reducedMotion` key),

  per-surface enable seams stay shipped defaults.

  disable when sound is off. Carries its own `EditorResolvedLayoutReport` (controls asserted
  against the modal, per the result-gate lesson).

  toggles everywhere (documented collision: Esc during an armed keyboard drag also opens it).
