# Theme — The Last Hour (frame v0.1, 2026-07-22)

**Frame (ADR 0010, decided):** The multiverse is dying, timeline by timeline. Outside time
stands **the Tower**, and at its final hour it recruits champions from every *when* —
bronze-age shieldmaids to orbital gunners — to descend into dying eras and fight for what
sand remains. Fantasy and sci-fi coexist by law, not by accident: the future is just
another era being eaten.

## The binding laws (the anti-scattershot answer)
1. **Everything references time.** Visual motif: clockwork, sand, astral decay. The
   overtime storm is *the Hour running out*. Replay/re-simulation is time-scrying.
2. **Era-capped units.** Each champion is ONE era played completely straight — the knight
   is a knight, the gunslinger is a gunslinger. The clash lives *between* units, never
   inside one. **v1 law: 8 champions, 8 eras, no repeats.**
3. **The Tower is the constant.** Hub outside time: shops after every node are the Tower's
   door behind you. Each expedition carries an **Hourstone**, a tablet cut from the Tower
   that binds its era-spanning champions to one shared Hour.

## Mechanics ↔ fiction (candidates — Jake steers)
- **Asymmetrical PvE = a timeline's death throes**: the consuming Hour does not recruit a
  fair warband. It produces swarms, engines, artillery, rituals, impossible bodies, and
  era-scale catastrophes. Encounter rules express how this particular history is dying.
- **Acts = eras**: each act is a descent into one dying era — acts gain visual, enemy, and
  boss identity and stop being numbers. Exact count is reopened by ADR 0016.
- **The final boss = the era-eater / Last Hour made manifest** (exact identity open).
  Defeating it is a completed run; continuing beyond it is a deliberate step outside the
  authored victory.
- **Endless = Beyond the Hour** (candidate): the Tower keeps the victorious warband in the
  collapse until some deeper timeline finally breaks its engine.
- **Inscriptions = stolen laws of dying timelines:** the Tower etches them into the
  expedition's Hourstone. Every bound champion obeys every acquired rule until the stone
  is sealed or fractures with the run.
- **Future PvP = Echoes**: optional no-stakes exhibitions against brother-timeline
  warbands. Echoes are compatible with the fiction but are not part of the core run.
- **Wager tiers → timefront stability**: Safe/Even/Greedy → **Stable / Fraying /
  Collapsing** (tier names were placeholder, ADR 0007 — these are the candidates).
- **Currency → Sand** (candidate; "gold" was explicitly a placeholder until theme landed —
  ADR 0006).
- Overtime storm → **the Waning** (candidate).

## The salvage spine (v0 candidates, 2026-07-28 — steering material, NOT locked)

Chat-landed with Jake; recorded as candidates, deliberately un-final:

- **Expedition motive (nodded):** descend into dying eras and salvage what can still be
  carried — heroes, gear, laws, sand. All of it feeds the Tower.
- **The Tower's purpose (deliberately vague):** the harvest **powers something** against the
  end of time. Candidates: seeding a second creation ("the Dawn") · sustaining a Tower that
  exists between timelines · something stranger. Do not lock yet.
- **The enemy has an author (nodded):** the collapse has a face. Act bosses are its
  manifestations in each era; the final boss is the closest a mortal expedition ever gets to
  the thing itself; it is never fully killable — victory tears this era's harvest from its
  jaws. Name open (the "gnawing" motif already lives in encounter names).
- **Act-boss extraction (Jake: "kinda cool"):** each act win extracts a **key resource beyond
  sand** — the era's heart/law/ember — fed to the Tower. For now this is fiction wearing the
  existing BossReward beat; a real cross-run meta layer is a **post-slice decision** and must
  respect the account-power NEVER law (unlock/narrative progression is the safe lane, not
  power).
- **Battlefield fiction (working):** a battle happens on the **last coherent shard of a dying
  era**, scried through the Hourstone. The Waning is the author's attention arriving; the
  Tower is visible from every shard because it neighbors them all.
- **Trapdoors (deliberately unwritten, nodded):** the Tower's own past · whether the Tower
  and the author of the collapse are connected.
- **Tone lean:** melancholy with Sand-warm hope (candidate; matches the Hall's approved
  cold-structure / warm-Sand material law).

## Class / champion split (ADR 0010, decided)
**Class** = readable function, survives as-is: Bulwark, Cleric, Shade, Sharpshot,
Pyromancer, Berserker, Phalanx, Banneret. **Champion** = the era-character wearing the
class. Deep dives design the class mechanics; champion lore/names layer on top.
