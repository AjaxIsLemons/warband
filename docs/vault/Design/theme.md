# Theme — The Last Hour (frame v0.1, 2026-07-22)

**Frame (ADR 0010, decided):** The multiverse is dying, timeline by timeline. Outside time
stands **the Tower**, and at its final hour it recruits champions from every *when* —
bronze-age shieldmaids to orbital gunners — to descend into dying eras and fight for what
sand remains. Fantasy and sci-fi coexist by law, not by accident: the future is just
another era being eaten.

## The binding laws (the anti-scattershot answer)
1. **Everything references time.** Visual motif: clockwork, sand, astral decay. The
   overtime storm is *the Hour running out*. Replay/re-simulation is time-scrying.
2. **Era-capped units.** Each champion is ONE era played completely straight — the knight
   is a knight, the gunslinger is a gunslinger. The clash lives *between* units, never
   inside one. **v1 law: 8 champions, 8 eras, no repeats.**
3. **The Tower is the constant.** Hub outside time: shops after every node are the Tower's
   door behind you; your banner is the Tower-issued mark that unifies an era-spanning
   warband on the board.

## Mechanics ↔ fiction (candidates — Jake steers)
- **Ghost bosses = Echoes**: warbands of brother-timelines, met at each era's gate. The
  best-of-5 record is your run's stand against five Echoes. Bot-ghosts: "when no echo
  answers, the Tower dreams one."
- **Acts = eras**: each act is a descent into one dying era (5 per run) — acts gain visual
  and monster identity, stop being numbers.
- **Wager tiers → timefront stability**: Safe/Even/Greedy → **Stable / Fraying /
  Collapsing** (tier names were placeholder, ADR 0007 — these are the candidates).
- **Currency → Sand** (candidate; "gold" was explicitly a placeholder until theme landed —
  ADR 0006).
- Overtime storm → **the Waning** (candidate).

## Class / champion split (ADR 0010, decided)
**Class** = readable function, survives as-is: Bulwark, Cleric, Shade, Sharpshot,
Pyromancer, Berserker, Phalanx, Banneret. **Champion** = the era-character wearing the
class. Deep dives design the class mechanics; champion lore/names layer on top.
