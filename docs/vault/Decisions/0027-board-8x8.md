# 0027 — The board is 8×8 (was 6×8), because the shape capped the camera

**Date:** 2026-07-28 · **Status:** accepted (Jake, in chat) · **Owner:** item 22

## Decision

`Battle.BoardCols` goes 6 → 8. Rows stay 8. The player half stays `row < BoardRows / 2`
(now 32 seats, was 24). Authored enemy formations are **semantically remapped**, not
re-authored. New camera defaults come from the sim-render audit's dial point.

## Why

[[sim-render-audit]] §3.2–3.3 measured it: a 0.91:1 board fills a 16:9 frame only at
pitch ~31°, and at any pitch that low every unit's nameplate stack smears across ~2–3 of
the rows behind it. The shape mathematically forbids a readable pitch. 8×8 at ~45° is the
only sweep point that fills the frame (98%) **and** halves the occlusion (2.95 → 1.37
rows). The rhombus instinct was answered there too: yaw costs frame area, and the
pointy-top zigzag already provides depth separation — so the fix is sim width, not camera
rotation.

## The remap law (how 6-wide formations became 8-wide)

Column map **{0→0, 1→1, 2→3, 3→4, 4→6, 5→7}** — edges stay edges, centers stay centers,
mirrored pairs stay mirrored around the new center (3.5). Hand-fix any cluster whose
adjacency the map breaks; adjacency is part of a formation's identity. No new bodies, no
new patterns — the content doctrine's cap holds. `BuildSweep`'s standardized duel is a
pure +1 translation instead (relative geometry preserved, so its metrics move only from
the width itself).

## Camera

New `tuning.json` defaults: **fov 34 · pitch 42 · yaw 6 · distance 1.45** (audit §3.4).
All four are F1 sliders with hot reload — these are starting positions for Jake's next
play pass, not commitments.

## Provenance

`Catalog.ContentVersion` already hashes every encounter position, so the remap changes
the fingerprint and `RunController.Resume` refuses old saves loudly. Board dims are now
hashed in as well, so a future dims-only change cannot silently diverge a save. The
replay wire format is untouched (stays v9 — positions were never width-bounded).

## Balance law (ADR 0016)

Justified on framing, **measured** for balance: `make baseline` before (byte-stable,
content `a6d4b52f`) and after (content `28b51d86`), diff read and published on the
board. Drift is expected — wider board favors mobility/kiting slightly — and is
*recorded, not tuned*. Named outliers in the roadmap's guard list get re-measured,
nothing gets rebalanced before playtest data says so.

## The one measured casualty (open, Jake's call)

**The act-1 boss admits 2 answer axes on the 8-wide board, was 3** (`make boss`:
balanced+damage; control fell 100%→0%). Isolated by experiment: control fails at the
remapped pair, at a 6-col stretch, at a centered 5-col gap, AND at the literal 6-wide
coordinates of both the pair and the player formations — so the width itself flipped it
(routing through the new columns + pathing tie-breaks), not any placement choice, and no
remap can restore the old exam. The Bond's other measured identity survives: enrage
fires ~100% and placement still swings 0↔100 within a lineup (`make oath`). The 2026-07
guard says bosses admit 3–4 axes; options are re-authoring the Oath for the wide board
(DESIGN), amending the guard for act 1 (it is the teaching boss), or waiting for
playtest data. Parked in the roadmap's design backlog — not tuned here, per doctrine.
