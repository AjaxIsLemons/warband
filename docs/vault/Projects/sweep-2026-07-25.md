# Outlier sweep — 2026-07-25 (post ADR 0022)

Re-run of the 2026-07-23 harness after the unit-behavior / weapon-cadence / signature-patch /
Frenzy changes. **The build matrix is comparable** (same construction: 64 builds, hero + 2 escorts,
rank S, Honed mastered). **The tier table is NOT** — the run layer changed shape between the two
sweeps (ADR 0019: three acts × five beats, Sand economy), so gold and boss-win figures measure a
different game and are recorded here only as a fresh baseline.

Sweep doctrine unchanged: the job is to **NAME**, not to tune.

Two predictions from the review landed as stated: the classes named as structurally broken by the
shared brain (Sharpshot, Pyromancer) are the two that moved most, and **Banneret did not move at
all** — its problem is that 100% of its output is an ally multiplier on a board that starts at three
units. Tuning will not reach that.

## Flags

- **DEAD `shade:*+reaper+widowmaker` (8–9%)** — NEW. Reaper builds cratered. Most likely cause: the
  daggers' cadence cut (10 → 5 mana/swing) halves Backstab's rate on the one chassis whose signature
  carries its damage. **Named, not tuned** — placeholder magnitude, and the fix is one number
  whenever Jake wants it.
- **CHASSIS-DEAD banneret (best build 19%)** — carried over, unchanged, now confirmed structural.
- **DEAD `banneret:warcaller+*+bearer` (5–8%)** — still confounded (Bearer is null by construction:
  no Inscriptions in the matrix).
- NODE-LOPSIDED `shade.reaper` vs `shade.phantom` Δ−67 (was −58) · `bulwark.juggernaut` vs
  `warden` Δ−48 (was −49) · `sharpshot.sniper.onebreath` vs `killwindow` Δ−42 (was −35) ·
  `phalanx.pikewall` vs `lancer` Δ+32 (was +26).
- **No chassis-DOMINANT build this time.** The 07-23 run flagged four (two Warden, two Phantom, all
  ≥91%); the top build is now 86% and the top eight span 77–86 instead of 84–94.
