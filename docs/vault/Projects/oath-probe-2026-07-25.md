## What changed: the Oathbound now stand symmetric

The 07-24 probe proved the Bulwark died first in **1000/1000** fights, so the encounter's own
pitch ("choose which threat you are willing to leave enraged") never occurred. The cause was
geometry, not numbers: the pair was placed **asymmetrically** — Bulwark at (row 5, col 2),
Sharpshot tucked behind at (6, 4). The Sharpshot was *structurally unreachable first*, so no
formation could ever choose it, and the Bulwark ate every opening.

The fix is a data change only — one line per enemy in `Encounters.BondedPair()`:

| | 07-24 (asymmetric) | now (edge mirror) |
|---|---|---|
| Oathbound Bulwark | row 5, col 2 | **row 5, col 0** |
| Oathbound Sharpshot | row 6, col 4 | **row 5, col 5** |

Both now stand on the same rank, hugging opposite board edges — a left threat and a right
threat the player can read off the deployment preview and *pick a side against*.

**Four placements were measured before shipping this one** (probe re-runs in ~3 s):

| placement | choice exists? | Δ win% | winning arrangements | bot-run smoke |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| (5,2)+(6,4) — 07-24 asymmetric | **no** — Bulwark always dies first | — | 2/32 | pass |
| (5,2)+(5,3) — center adjacent | yes | 72 | 11/32 | **FAIL** |
| (5,1)+(5,4) — wide mirror | yes | 87 | 8/32 | **FAIL** |
| **(5,0)+(5,5) — edge mirror (shipped)** | **yes** | **84** | 10/32 | **pass** |

The two inner-symmetric variants make the boss hard enough that the bot policy loses act 1 in
4 of 6 seeded runs (`ContentTests.FullRunsCompleteOnRealContent`). The edge mirror poses the
same decision without that: separating the pair to the board edges lets a formation commit to
one flank *and get there*, instead of being caught between two threats that overlap.

## Is the Bond real?

- Enrage fires in **81%** of fights.
- When it fires, the fight runs **71.2 more ticks** and the survivor throws **10.5 more swings**.

> LIVE — the survivor gets real swings out of the Enrage. The 'which one do I leave standing' question has teeth.

## What this means (and what it does not)

**Fixed:** the encounter now poses its own pitch. Both Oathbound survive in real fights, the
survivor is chosen by where the player stands, and the two branches cost very different
amounts (Δ84). Board item 3 is closed.

**Named, not tuned** — three things a future session or playtest should look at, none of them
touched here:

1. **The decision has a strongly correct answer.** Leaving the Sharpshot enraged wins 16%;
   leaving the Bulwark enraged wins 100%. That is a *lesson* ("kill the archer first"), which
   is legitimate for a first act boss that teaches — `pve-encounters.md` says the boss
   mechanically rules and teaches its act — but it is not yet a genuine two-answer dilemma.
   Whether act 1 wants a lesson or a dilemma is Jake's call, not the probe's.
2. **A third outcome exists and is invisible to the player:** four arrangements kill both
   Oathbound close enough together that Enrage never fires at all (`enrage% = 0`). That is the
   cleanest possible answer to the encounter, and nothing in the UI names it. Worth a callout
   in the result report if it survives play.
3. **Two arrangements run very long** (`bulw+pyro+cler | bait-left` at 387 ticks,
   `pyro+shar+cler | right-stack` at 381) — an enraged Sharpshot kiting a comp that cannot
   close. Not a stall (both resolve), but it is the shape that Overtime exists to end.

**Unchanged caveats from 07-24:** formations assign slots by lineup order, so a formation name
is only strictly comparable within a lineup; every hero runs its STARTER weapon at rank 0 with
no spec nodes, leaving the Planning screen's 3-weapons-per-hero axis unexplored.
