# Boss probe — the act bosses as strength exams

6 formations × 24 seeds, four answer-axis parties, each sized to its act (rank C→A, forked from act 2). Crit is the sim's only RNG, so seeds are the whole distribution.

**Three bars, not one.** ① it must be an exam (not free) · ② placement must still move the result · ③ **more than one axis must be able to pass it**, or the boss is prescribing a build (pve-encounters.md).
