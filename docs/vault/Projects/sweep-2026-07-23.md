## Interpretation (the sweep's job is to NAME, not tune)

### A · Real mechanical outliers (design attention)
1. **Phase uptime is near-degenerate.** Phantom × Here-and-Gone ≈ Phase every cast
   (15t) on a fast-swinging, fast-casting chassis → roughly 70-80% untargetable+immune
   uptime. All four phantom builds sit 86-94%; reaper-vs-phantom Δ-58 is the game's
   biggest node gap. Candidate levers AT TUNE TIME: Phase internal cooldown, shorter
   here-and-gone duration, or mana-gain lockout while Phased. NOT touched.
2. **Warden Taunt owns small fights.** Team-wide forced-targeting + silence at r3-4
   vs 3-unit boards = 84-92% for all four warden builds (juggernaut Δ-49 behind).
   Partly harness-scale (Taunt value scales with % of enemy board caught); still the
   #2 watch item.
3. **Banneret floor (5-20%)** — CONFOUNDED: a solo support in a stat-stick matrix,
   and Bearer is null by construction (no banners in the matrix). But even with
   mana-bearing escorts the whole class sits bottom by a wide margin; his numbers
   deserve first look whenever tuning starts.

### B · Harness-shaped (discount before reacting)
Pikewall's Δ26 (counters feast on all-melee escorts) · Bearer's 5-9% (see above) ·
59 non-draw mirrors — understood: odd-r hex parity means mirrored placements aren't
geometrically identical, and both teams share one RNG stream (id-order crit rolls);
same-seed reruns are bit-identical, so determinism is intact.

### C · Placeholder-tuning signals (explicitly out of scope, recorded only)
Victory saturates ~99-100% at every tier → bot ghosts + monsters are undertuned;
tiers can't differentiate on winrate, so **Greedy is strictly dominant on gold
(708 vs 383) at zero risk** — the difficulty curve is the real fix, not the payout.
One Breath still bottom-8 even after the true-double fix (slow-huge loses value when
fights end fast). Pyromancer lowish overall (DoT ramp vs short placeholder fights).
